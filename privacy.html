Privacy notice

How personal information is handled on xeffic.com and app.xeffic.com.

Last updated: 28 September 2026. This notice covers the public website and the invitation-only Xeffic RFQ pilot. Contact us for details of a proposed pilot deployment before submitting confidential customer files.

1. Who is responsible

Dr Hirbod Varasteh, trading as Xeffic, is currently a sole trader. Xeffic is not an incorporated company. For business correspondence or to request a postal address, email hirbod@xeffic.com for privacy requests.

Hirbod Varasteh is the controller for public-site enquiries, business correspondence, pilot account administration and security. Where an organisation uploads RFQs or drawings containing personal information for its own purposes, it normally acts as controller and Xeffic acts as processor under its written instructions. We will help the organisation handle data-subject requests about that content. This division depends on the actual arrangement.

2. Information and sources

  • Public-site enquiries: name, email, organisation, topic, message and correspondence supplied through the contact form or email. The form sends data through Formspree.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser/device details, timestamps, requested pages, diagnostics and security events collected by the site or pilot infrastructure.
  • Pilot users: work email, organisation, sign-in/session records, workspace membership, user actions, support correspondence and audit records.
  • Customer documents: uploaded RFQs, engineering drawings, specifications and supporting files, extracted requirements, flags, quotations, clarifications, costing data, decisions and downloaded packs. They may incidentally contain names, signatures or other personal information.

Information comes from you, your organisation, your use of the services and our service providers. Provide only information needed for the enquiry or pilot. Do not upload special-category data, payment-card details or unnecessary personal information without a separately agreed basis and safeguards.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

PurposeBasis where Xeffic is controller
Answer enquiries and arrange demonstrationsLegitimate interests in responding and developing business relationships; steps toward a contract where the individual is a party
Provide accounts, sign-in and pilot supportLegitimate interests in delivering the customer's authorised service; contract where applicable

When Xeffic is processor for customer content, the customer decides its lawful basis and Xeffic handles the content only for the agreed service and documented instructions. We do not make solely automated decisions about individuals with legal or similarly significant effects.

4. AI-assisted analysis

The pilot analyses submitted engineering material to propose requirements, possible inconsistencies, clarifications and draft review or costing information. A human engineer or estimator must check the original documents and final decision. Before customer files are uploaded, the customer should obtain a written description of the current AI supplier, processing location, retention and model-training settings. Xeffic will not claim that data is excluded from model training until the production configuration and supplier terms are verified.

5. Recipients and transfers

Authorised Xeffic personnel and providers that operate hosting, storage, authentication/email, AI processing, security and support may access data as needed. The public site's form uses Formspree; Formspree states that it uses US infrastructure and processes information in the US and other countries. The website uses Cloudflare infrastructure. The providers used by the pilot depend on its production configuration. Please request its current supplier and subprocessor details before submitting customer files. We may disclose information to advisers or authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information.

Some suppliers may process information outside the UK. We will provide the current processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards to pilot customers before they submit confidential files. Contact us for details.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information only while needed for the purpose above, then delete or anonymise it subject to legal retention and backup cycles. Retention depends on the enquiry, active account, agreed pilot period and legal or security needs. We will provide the current periods for uploads, outputs, audit logs and backups in the pilot agreement before customer files are submitted. The customer's pilot agreement should define export, account closure, production deletion and backup expiry. A legal hold may extend retention where necessary.

7. Security

We restrict access to authorised users and service providers and use sign-in and other measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. No online system is risk free. Report suspected unauthorised access to hirbod@xeffic.com. Specific encryption, backup and incident commitments will be stated in the customer agreement after verification.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to the applicable rules and circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal information. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time. If we rely on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email us; we may need to verify identity. For personal information in customer-uploaded files, contact that customer organisation as controller.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. We welcome the opportunity to address your concern first.

9. Cookies and device storage

The pilot may use essential session and security storage to provide sign-in. The public website may use security-related storage through its host. A current inventory of storage names, providers and lifetimes is available on request; we will update this notice after the live configuration has been verified. Any non-essential storage or tracking must be handled under applicable UK rules, with an appropriate consent or objection control where required. You can change browser settings, but blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in.

10. Changes and future incorporation

We will update this notice when practices change. If a company later takes over Xeffic, we will identify the new legal entity, explain any controller change or data transfer, and update the notice and customer agreements before the change takes effect where required.
