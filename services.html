SERVICES

Engineering services designed for difficult, high-value problems.

From flood intelligence and energy systems to simulation and R&D support, Xeffic helps turn uncertainty into engineering evidence.

01
FLOOD AI

Flood intelligence and AI-assisted risk workflows

We develop decision-support concepts that bring together environmental data, engineering knowledge and AI methods to improve the speed and consistency of flood-risk screening.

  • Flood detection and event screening workflows
  • Satellite, rainfall, terrain and sensor-data integration concepts
  • AI-assisted prioritisation and alerting prototypes
  • Infrastructure and asset-level risk visualisation
  • Engineering validation of model assumptions
02
INDUSTRIAL ENERGY

Waste-heat recovery and thermal systems

We support industrial organisations in understanding where heat is being lost, how much can realistically be recovered and which system architecture is technically appropriate.

  • Waste-heat opportunity assessment
  • Heat-exchanger and thermal-system concepts
  • Thermodynamic and heat-transfer modelling
  • Thermosyphon and passive-loop concepts
  • Techno-economic screening

SIMULATION

FEA, CFD and engineering analysis

Simulation is used to explore design limits, failure modes and performance before expensive physical changes are made.

  • Finite element analysis
  • Computational fluid dynamics
  • Thermal and coupled-physics modelling
  • Load-path and serviceability assessment
  • Sensitivity and design-space studies

AI & DIGITAL ENGINEERING

AI-enabled engineering workflows

  • Engineering knowledge assistants
  • Automated technical-data triage
  • AI-assisted design and optimisation workflows
  • Survey-to-engineering automation concepts
  • Decision-support interfaces

CARBON & SUSTAINABILITY

Technical feasibility and process-level analysis for carbon reduction, capture, energy integration and sustainable technology concepts.

  • Research and feasibility support
