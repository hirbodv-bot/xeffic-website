Pilot terms of use

For authorised users of the Xeffic RFQ review pilot.

Last updated: 28 September 2026. Dr Hirbod Varasteh trades as Xeffic as a sole trader. These terms govern app.xeffic.com for authorised business users. A signed customer pilot agreement prevails where it conflicts with these online terms. Contact hirbod@xeffic.com.

1. Pilot and access

The pilot helps analyse RFQs, drawings and specifications, identify possible requirements or inconsistencies, prepare clarification and costing material, and generate review packs. It is a limited evaluation service. Access is for users authorised by their organisation. Use your own work email, protect one-time sign-in links and codes, and report suspected misuse. The customer is responsible for deciding which staff may use its workspace and promptly telling Xeffic when access should end.

2. Customer content and confidentiality

The customer retains its rights in uploaded materials and gives Xeffic and approved service providers the limited permission needed to host, process and generate review outputs for the agreed pilot. The customer must have rights and a lawful basis to submit the material and should remove unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Xeffic will use customer confidential information only to provide, secure and support the pilot, restrict access to those who need it, and not disclose it outside approved providers except with permission or as required by law. A signed confidentiality and data-processing agreement should specify permitted suppliers, locations, instructions and security measures before live confidential files are used.

3. AI output and engineer approval

AI suggestions, flags, evidence links, draft calculations and costing may be wrong, incomplete or misleading. Users must check the original documents, drawing revisions, units, quantities, tolerances, materials, specifications, assumptions, calculations, prices, lead times and contract terms. “Mark ready for estimate” and “Approve RFQ” are internal statuses; neither issues a quotation nor certifies a design. The customer's authorised engineer or estimator remains responsible for the final engineering, safety, procurement and commercial decision. The pilot must not be the sole control for a safety-critical or regulated decision.

4. Acceptable use

Do not access another organisation's workspace, share codes, upload malware, bypass controls, interfere with operation, reverse engineer the service or use it unlawfully. Do not upload special-category personal data, payment-card information or export-controlled information without an explicit written arrangement. We may suspend access where reasonably necessary for security or law, notifying the customer where practicable.

5. Ownership and feedback

Xeffic retains its software, underlying methods and branding. The customer may use its outputs internally for the agreed evaluation, subject to rights in source material and its written agreement. Suggestions voluntarily given about the pilot may inform product improvements, but Xeffic must not reveal customer confidential information or use customer documents to train a general model without separate express agreement and verified supplier controls.

6. Pilot duration, fees and support

The signed pilot agreement should state start and end dates, users, fees if any, support, availability and any service levels. In the absence of a written commitment, the evaluation may change and uninterrupted access or error-free output is not promised. Do not infer a free or perpetual licence from access to the workspace.

7. Exit, data and liability

The written pilot agreement should set the process for export, closure, deletion, backups, incident reporting, confidentiality, allocation of liability and dispute resolution. These online terms do not replace an Article 28 data-processing agreement where Xeffic is a processor. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded, including fraud or death or personal injury caused by negligence. English and Welsh law governs, subject to mandatory law.

8. Legal identity changes

If Xeffic is later incorporated, the new company does not automatically replace Dr Hirbod Varasteh as the contracting party. Any transfer or replacement of the customer agreement will be handled expressly, with related privacy information updated.
