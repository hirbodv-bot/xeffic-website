THE PROBLEM

Too much engineering time is spent finding information before quotation can begin.

RFQ packages can spread critical requirements across emails, drawings, specifications and supporting documents. Xeffic helps organise that first review while keeping engineering judgement in the loop.

Find requirements

Structure quantities, materials, revisions, finishes and other technical requirements from the enquiry package.

Surface gaps and conflicts

Highlight missing information and inconsistencies that may need clarification before a reliable quotation.

Keep evidence traceable

Connect review findings back to source documents so an engineer can verify the output rather than accept a black-box answer.
