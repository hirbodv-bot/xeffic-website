# Xeffic legal pages: publication checks

The website pages have an interim version for the current sole trader business. At the owner’s request, the public address is deferred. This leaves a legal disclosure gap for the Xeffic trading name; add a serviceable address as soon as one is available. The pilot supplier, retention and storage details also need verification before confidential customer files are submitted. The app at app.xeffic.com is a separate codebase and must link to the public privacy and pilot terms on its unauthenticated sign-in screen and in the workspace.

## Outstanding follow-up

1. **Operator and address.** Confirm “Dr Hirbod Varasteh trading as Xeffic” is the correct business identity. Choose a genuine geographic business/contact address where correspondence can reach you and legal documents can be served; do not put a residential address online without consciously deciding to do so. Put it in all three pages. Use your name and trading name on invoices and business correspondence. Do not add “Ltd” or a company number until incorporation.
2. **Production data map.** List the real pilot's hosting, object storage, email/sign-in, AI/model, security, logging, backups and support suppliers. Record what each sees, UK/overseas locations, onward suppliers, training and human-review settings, access controls and transfer safeguards. Add the verified names or suitably precise categories to the Privacy notice and customer agreement.
3. **Retention.** Choose explicit periods for contact enquiries, dormant user accounts, uploaded documents, generated outputs, audit/security logs, one-time codes and backups. Confirm deletion behaviour in code and suppliers before filling the notice. Make export/deletion terms in the customer agreement consistent.
4. **Cookies and browser storage.** Audit both domains in a clean browser before and after sign-in. Record name, provider, purpose and lifetime; decide whether each is strictly necessary or uses another applicable exception. Implement an appropriate consent or objection mechanism for non-exempt uses under current ICO guidance.
5. **Customer agreement.** For Ashtead or another business customer, sign a pilot order with scope, duration, price, support, confidentiality, liability, security, exit and deletion. Where Xeffic processes personal data under its instructions, attach an Article 28 data-processing agreement with the processing schedule, documented instructions, subprocessor authorisation, international transfers, security, assistance, breaches, audit and deletion/return. Online pilot terms alone do not supply those details.
6. **Contact form.** The public site currently posts to Formspree. Confirm its recipient, retention and terms; test a submission after deployment.
7. **Business administration.** Check HMRC Self Assessment obligations and use the ICO data-protection-fee checker for the sole trader's actual processing. These are separate from publishing a Privacy notice.
8. **App links.** Add `https://xeffic.com/privacy.html` and `https://xeffic.com/pilot-terms.html` to the app's sign-in screen and authenticated navigation. If users must accept terms, record version and acceptance time. Do not treat a privacy notice as consent to all processing.
9. **Review.** Have a UK solicitor review the customer agreement and online legal terms before relying on them for a live confidential pilot. Set the displayed update date to the actual publication date.

## If a limited company is registered later

Do not silently change “Xeffic” to the company as contracting party. Update the legal name, company number, registered office and contact details on the site, contracts, invoices and Privacy notice. Decide whether contracts and data move to the company and document any novation or assignment and privacy/controller notices as appropriate. Review insurance, taxes, supplier accounts and the ICO registration/fee position for the new entity.
